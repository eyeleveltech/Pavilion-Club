The club

The Pavilion Club is a pickleball club, not a sports complex. Three courts under one roof, and everything around them done properly: a tournament-grade surface, light that never loses the ball, and a front desk that picks up the phone.

Surface

Tournament-grade acrylic, laid to regulation lines.

Light

LED lighting under the pergola roof, shielded to keep glare off the ball.

Desk

Staffed from first serve to last rally, every day from 06:00 AM.

01
01 — The courts

The
Courts

Three courts laid to tournament dimensions under one pergola roof. An acrylic surface, nets at 34 inches at the centre, and sides open to the trees.

02 — The evening

The
Evening

Play runs to eleven on weeknights and midnight on weekends. The lights are LED, mounted high and shielded, so the ball stays visible at the top of a lob.

03 — The club

The
Club

Cool down at Rally & Brew, do the strength work in the wellness centre, book the banquet hall upstairs for the tournament after-party. It is a club, not a corridor of courts.

What's here

One address. the whole evening.

Everything you need before, during and after a session sits under one roof. Only the courts are booked online.
