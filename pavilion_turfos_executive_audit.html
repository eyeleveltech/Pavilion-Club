System Architecture & Platform Topology

The Dual-Engine Architectural Model

The Pavilion Club does not run on a monolithic website. It is engineered with a strict Headless Luxury Architecture where customer experience is completely decoupled from database state, inventory controls, and financial transactions.

Engine 1: Public Web Portal

Live · Port 3001

High-performance Next.js application built with bespoke luxury editorial aesthetics, optimized for mobile booking, membership discovery, and instant enquiry capture.

  • Static & SSR edge rendering for ultra-fast load times.
  • Zero hardcoded API secrets (Keyless Public API client).
  • Local state persistence for instant desk operations.
  • Dedicated Studio Rate Card & Interactive Timetables.

Engine 2: Turf-OS Core Platform

Live · Port 3000

Central club operating system hosting PostgreSQL multi-tenant isolation, inventory locks, payment verification, pack credit ledgers, and front-desk POS consoles.

  • Tenant ID: 1a752910-5403-4539-b58c-db585ed57ddc
  • Atomic 10-minute hold locking (zero double-bookings).
  • Credit pack deduction and session balance engine.
  • Background notification queues & Razorpay webhooks.

Data Flow Between Public Portal & Turf-OS

1
Public Inventory Query: Public site requests real-time court availability via GET /api/public/v1/availability?date=YYYY-MM-DD. Turf-OS inspects active bookings, blackout slots, and operating hours.
2
Atomic Slot Hold: When a customer selects a slot, POST /api/public/v1/holds locks the court for 10 minutes, generating an immutable hold token.
3
Payment Execution: Customer either pays online via Razorpay order or selects Pay-at-Venue. A webhook or desk confirmation converts the hold into a verified booking with a cryptographically signed Pass QR code.

Turf-OS Core Engine & Database Architecture

PostgreSQL Core Schema & Ledgers

  • tenants: Isolation domain for Pavilion Club (branding, slug, timezone).
  • venues & courts: Mogappair court definitions, sports, and court labels.
  • bookings & holds: Slot inventory, holds (10-min expiry), status, check-in flags.
  • pack_products: Official membership and studio packs catalog.
  • customer_packs: Ledger tracking purchased hours, remaining credits, and expiry.
  • pack_redemptions: Audit trail mapping specific bookings to credit debits.
  • enquiries: Inbound leads across Studio, Academy, and Memberships.
  • customers: Verified player identity, phone index, and email links.

Turf-OS Central API Gateway (/api/public/v1/*)

Endpoint Method Payload / Query Functionality
/availability GET ?date=YYYY-MM-DD Calculates open court inventory, slot prices, and booking fee in paise.
/holds POST {court_id, starts_at, customer} Acquires atomic 10-minute hold lock to prevent double booking.
/bookings/:id/pay POST {booking_id} Initializes server-side Razorpay order and returns client key ID.
/bookings/:id/confirm-at-venue POST {booking_id} Settles booking for pay-at-venue venues without online gateway.
/customers/:phone/packs GET :phone Returns customer's active pack products and remaining credit balance.
/bookings/:id/redeem-pack POST {pack_id} Debits 1 credit from customer pack and instantly confirms court slot.
/enquiries POST {type, name, phone, email, notes} Central lead capture. Creates customer entity and inserts into CRM table.
/me/code & /me/verify POST {email, code} Issues cryptographic JWT session token for customer self-service.

End-to-End Operational Workflows

Workflow A: The Physical Onboarding & Signature Flow (Phase 4)

Designed in direct alignment with the client's audio brief with Dharshini. Connects digital web discovery to physical front-desk onboarding with zero technical friction.

1
Digital Discovery: Prospective member browses /membership/#studio and clicks "Apply & Enquire Online" on their preferred programme (e.g. Salsa Couple Package).
2
Dynamic Lead Capture: The modal opens with programme name, fee (₹5,000 + GST), and cohort pre-filled. Customer inputs name, phone (+91), email, and submits.
3
Dual Dispatch: Lead is stored in Turf-OS database and local storage. Customer receives immediate confirmation with reference number (e.g. #STU-2026-4819).
4
Physical A4 Sheet Printing: Front desk staff at /admin/enquiries clicks the 🖨️ Printer icon to generate the formatted admission sheet (or customer prints their own copy).
5
On-Ground Execution & Spot QR Payment: When the member visits the club:
  • Member signs Section 4 of the printed form (Member Signature & Date).
  • Front desk collects payment manually using counter UPI QR code or card machine.
  • Staff enters transaction ID and signs Section 5 (Desk Officer Reconciliation Slip).
  • Desk activates member pack in Turf-OS Front Desk console.

Workflow B: Member Pack Credit Burn (Court Booking)

Automated credit deduction that allows members who hold an active pack (e.g. 20-session Pickleball pack) to book courts without paying per session.

1
Phone Lookup: Customer selects court slot at /book and types their registered mobile number.
2
Pack Recognition: System calls /customers/:phone/packs. If the customer has unexpired credits matching the sport, a golden "Redeem from Active Pack" option appears instead of the payment button.
3
Instant Confirmation: System calls /redeem-pack, deducts 1 session from ledger, updates customer_packs.credits_used, and generates the digital pass instantly.

Pending Deliverables & Strategic Roadmap

Executive Roadmap for Pavilion Club Leadership

To ensure total operational independence so that the club management never requires engineering assistance for daily operations, promotional campaigns, or pricing adjustments, the following items are scheduled:

Immediate & Near-Term Deliverables (Prioritized)

1. Official SMTP Mailbox Credentials for Automated Email Dispatch Immediate Action

Current State: Enquiries are captured instantly in Turf-OS database and the Front Desk web console.
Action Required: Club management needs to provide SMTP host, username, and password for frontdesk@thepavilionclub.com (or enter them into http://localhost:3000/settings/email). Once saved, every online submission will automatically trigger an email notification directly to the front desk mailbox.

2. Front Desk Rate Card & Timetable CMS Phase 5 (Next Sprint)

Objective: Allow the club owner or manager to update programme pricing, batch timings, class counts, and coach names directly from the Turf-OS Admin UI without touching frontend code. Includes a 1-click "Publish to Website" action.

3. Promotional Posters & Hero Banner Manager Phase 5 (Next Sprint)

Objective: File uploader within Turf-OS settings where new graphic posters (such as the Inaugural Pickleball ₹2,000 poster or upcoming holiday tournaments) can be uploaded and toggled active on the homepage hero or announcement popup.

4. Meta WhatsApp Cloud API Integration (Automated Bot) Mid-Term Deliverable

Context: As discussed in the Dharshini audio brief, direct WhatsApp automation requires Facebook Meta Business verification. Currently, the system uses friction-free direct WhatsApp chat links (https://wa.me/...). Once Meta verification is completed, automated template dispatches (booking passes, payment confirmations, and batch reminders) will be wired.

5. Biometric / QR Turnstile Hardware Integration Future Strategic

Objective: Connect the cryptographically signed Pass QR code directly to physical turnstile barrier gates at the club entry for fully automated, unmanned check-in.
